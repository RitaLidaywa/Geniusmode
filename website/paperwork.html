What Paperwork is for

Paperwork by tiri is for the operational and professional documents your business runs on. Standard operating procedures, internal policies, contract templates, service agreements, and onboarding paperwork. I write these in plain, direct language that your team can actually follow and your clients can actually sign.

This is for the business owner who's been running on verbal agreements too long, the team that keeps re-explaining the same process, or the founder who needs a contract template before the next client call.

What's included

  • An intake session to understand your process, risk points, and existing documents
  • A first draft structured for clarity and ease of use, not legal jargon
  • One round of revisions to align with how your business actually operates
  • A final, ready-to-use document formatted for signing or internal rollout
  • A plain-language note on where to get formal legal review if needed

How it works

I write operational and process documents, not legal contracts requiring a licensed attorney's sign-off. For anything that needs formal legal certification, I'll flag that clearly and point you toward review. Most single documents are ready within three to five business days.
