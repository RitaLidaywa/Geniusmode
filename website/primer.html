Turning what you know into something people can learn.

What Primer is for

Primer by tiri is for turning expertise into something teachable. Course modules, onboarding guides, explainer content, workshop material, and how-to documentation. I take what's in your head, ask the questions a beginner would ask, and structure it so it actually lands.

This is for the coach who has the knowledge but not the curriculum, the founder who needs onboarding docs that don't require a live walkthrough every time, or the expert whose explanations only make sense to other experts.

What's included

  • A structured intake call to map what you know and who you're teaching it to
  • A learning outline that sequences the material logically
  • Full draft content written in plain, teachable language
  • One round of revisions to sharpen clarity and tone
  • A final, ready-to-deliver version formatted for your platform

How it works

We start with your subject matter and audience. I build an outline first so you can approve the structure before I write a single sentence. Then I draft, you review, and we revise once before final delivery. Timelines scale with scope, from a single explainer in a few days to a full curriculum over several weeks.
