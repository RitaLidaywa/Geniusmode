Prose by tiri

Writing that sounds like you, not like a template.

What Prose is for

Prose by tiri is for the writing that has to sound like a person, because it does. Personal essays, founder stories, blog posts, newsletters, LinkedIn content, and brand narratives. I interview you, pull out how you actually talk about your work, and write it down in a shape people want to read to the end.

This isn't content mill copywriting. It's for the piece you've been meaning to write for six months, the story your business needs to tell but you keep putting off, or the weekly writing you know matters but never have time for.

What's included

  • A discovery call or written brief to capture your voice, context, and goal for the piece
  • A first draft written entirely by me, in your voice, not a generic AI tone
  • One structured round of revisions based on your feedback
  • A final, publish-ready draft formatted for your platform of choice
  • A short style note so future pieces stay consistent

How it works

You send a brief or book a short call. I draft, you review, we revise once, and you get a final piece ready to publish. Turnaround is typically three to seven business days depending on scope.
