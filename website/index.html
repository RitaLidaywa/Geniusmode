About

I've been the person staring at the blank page, so I built a business around not leaving anyone there.

I started Tiri because I kept meeting people who knew exactly what they needed to say, and still couldn't get it onto the page. Writing isn't a talent problem for most people. It's a stuck problem.

I work on three fronts: writing prose that sounds like you, building primers that teach what you know, and drafting paperwork that protects what you've built. Alongside that, I run Pata Creative Studio, where I build small AI-powered products that solve one problem well.

Every project comes through me directly. No account managers, no hand-offs.

Think. Try. Teach. — Rita
