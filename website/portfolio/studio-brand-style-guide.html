Studio Brand Style Guide

A clear, single-source style guide that stopped three different logo versions from circulating across a growing creative studio's marketing.

ClientBoutique creative studio
TypeBrand documentation
Deliverable28-page brand style guide

The problem

As the studio brought on contractors, three different logo files and two conflicting color palettes were circulating across their marketing materials, with no single source of truth.

The approach

I audited every asset in use, consolidated with the founder on the correct versions, and documented logo usage, color codes, typography, and voice guidelines in one reference document built for contractors, not just internal staff.

The result

Every new contractor now receives the guide on day one, and inconsistent brand assets in client-facing work dropped to zero within a month.
