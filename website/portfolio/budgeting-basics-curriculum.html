Budgeting Basics Curriculum

A six-module course that turned a financial coach's scattered knowledge into a structured course her clients actually finished.

ClientIndependent financial coach
TypeCourse curriculum
Deliverable6 modules, 24 lessons, workbook

The problem

The coach had years of one-on-one client material but no packaged course. Past attempts to write it herself stalled because the material made sense in her head but not on the page.

The approach

I mapped her one-on-one process into six teachable modules, sequenced from mindset to mechanics to maintenance, then wrote 24 lessons in plain language with worksheets she could hand to any client without extra explanation.

The result

Her course completion rate in the first cohort was 78 percent, well above the 20 to 30 percent typical for self-paced courses in her space.
