Founder LinkedIn Series

Twelve weeks of ghostwritten LinkedIn posts that turned a quiet, technical founder into a name people in his industry started to recognise.

ClientSaaS founder, logistics tech
TypeGhostwritten LinkedIn content
Deliverable36 posts over 12 weeks

The problem

The founder had strong opinions and real expertise but no consistent presence. Every post attempt sat in drafts because he couldn't find the time or the voice to publish regularly.

The approach

I ran a single 90-minute voice interview, then wrote three posts a week for twelve weeks, each one built around a real opinion or story he'd shared. He approved posts in batches of three, keeping his weekly time cost under fifteen minutes.

The result

His profile went from under 200 followers to a recognised voice in his niche, with two inbound partnership conversations directly attributed to the series.
