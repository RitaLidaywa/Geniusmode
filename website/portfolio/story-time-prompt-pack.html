Pata Creative Studio

Story Time Prompt Pack

A 40-prompt library that helps time-strapped parents turn five spare minutes into a bedtime story their kids actually want to hear again.

ClientAI Kiddo (in-house product)
TypeDigital product / prompt library
DeliverablePDF pack + companion prompts

The problem

Parents wanted to read AI-assisted bedtime stories with their kids but didn't know how to prompt for age-appropriate, imaginative results without generic or repetitive output.

The approach

I built 40 structured prompts across five categories (adventure, animals, gentle life lessons, silly, and custom-character stories), each tested for consistent tone and age-appropriate pacing across common AI tools.

The result

The pack became one of Pata Creative Studio's top sellers in its first month, with parents reporting they used it multiple nights a week instead of once.
