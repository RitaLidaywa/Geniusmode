Logistics SOP Manual

A 60-page operating manual that cut new-hire training time from three weeks to five days for a growing last-mile delivery company.

ClientLast-mile delivery startup
TypeOperations documentation
Deliverable60-page SOP manual, 14 processes

The problem

The company had grown from 4 to 40 employees with no written processes. Every new hire learned by shadowing someone, which meant inconsistent handling and no way to scale training.

The approach

I shadowed operations for two days, interviewed team leads across dispatch, warehousing, and customer support, then documented 14 core processes in plain, step-by-step language with clear ownership and escalation paths.

The result

New-hire ramp time dropped from three weeks to five days, and the manual became the reference document used in every internal audit since.
